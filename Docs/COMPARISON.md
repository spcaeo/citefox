## Where CiteFox leads

- **Your data stays on your computer.** CiteFox is a desktop app. Projects, AI answers and reports stay in
  its local data folder and keys in the system keychain. Every other tool here is a hosted service.
- **No subscription.** The app is free. Checks run on your own API keys or credits, with the cost shown
  before each check and a monthly budget it will not pass.
- **The right page, not just the domain.** You set a Primary page (and Also OK pages) for each question
  or topic, and every answer is sorted into right page, another page of yours, named without a link, or
  someone else. No other tool's docs describe an expected page per prompt; Profound and Peec AI show which
  of your URLs were cited.
- **Fixes with a measured verdict.** Log a change and CiteFox re-checks the questions it should help, then
  says after 14 days whether it worked, made no change or got worse. Profound and Peec AI mark actions on
  a chart; neither gives a verdict.
- **Client reports in the box.** Six reports with your agency or company branding, as offline HTML, PDF or
  CSV, or emailed on a schedule, with no extra plan.
- **Source-available.** Anyone can read what it does and how each number is made.
- **No lock-in, no per-seat fees.** Export everything as HTML, PDF or CSV; track as many projects and
  clients as you like.

## Where others go further, and why CiteFox is built differently

- **A hosted dashboard for a team.** The others run in a browser: shared logins, client guest access,
  live dashboards and Looker Studio connectors. CiteFox runs on one computer and shares results as files
  and emails.
- **More engines.** Promptwatch, Scrunch, AthenaHQ and the Enterprise plans of Profound and Peec AI also
  track Claude, and several add Grok, DeepSeek, Meta AI, Mistral or more. CiteFox tracks six.
- **Prompt volume and large datasets.** Ahrefs Brand Radar researches any brand across an index of
  525 million+ prompts with history back to 2025; Profound, Peec AI, Promptwatch, Semrush and AthenaHQ
  estimate how often people ask a prompt. CiteFox only measures the questions you track.
- **AI crawler and AI traffic analytics.** Profound, Peec AI, Searchable, Scrunch, Ahrefs and (on higher
  plans) Otterly.AI and Promptwatch read server or CDN logs and AI referrals. CiteFox does not read
  traffic at all.
- **API, MCP and enterprise features.** Most others offer a public API, an MCP server, SSO and dedicated
  support. With CiteFox your data is already yours, in a local database you can query, export or
  back up; an MCP server for AI agents is coming soon.
- **Content writing.** Promptwatch, Searchable, Goodie AI and AthenaHQ have agents that write or refresh
  pages. CiteFox writes a brief; a person writes the page.
- **Run frequency.** Most others run prompts daily (Promptwatch up to hourly, Rankscale hourly to
  monthly). CiteFox's shortest interval is daily, and each check costs your own credits.

## Who should use what

- **An agency or marketer who wants page-level answers, fix verdicts and branded client reports without a
  subscription, and wants the data on their own computer:** CiteFox.
- **A large brand that needs many engines, prompt volumes, crawler logs, SSO and a dedicated team:**
  Profound, or an Enterprise plan of Peec AI or Scrunch.
- **A team that wants a hosted, self-serve tracker with daily runs and a low entry price:** Otterly.AI
  (from $29) or Peec AI.
- **A team that also wants an agent to write and publish content:** Promptwatch, Searchable or Goodie AI.
- **A team already paying for Semrush or Ahrefs:** their AI add-ons keep AI visibility beside keyword and
  backlink data; Ahrefs Brand Radar adds a large prompt index for researching any brand.
- **Both:** CiteFox sits beside any of these. A hosted tool can watch the whole category daily while
  CiteFox checks which of your pages is cited and whether each fix worked.

## Notes

- **Peec AI's engines.** Its pricing page lists ChatGPT, AI Mode, AI Overviews, Copilot, Gemini and Naver AI
  for the self-serve plans, of which a plan runs 3 at a time; Perplexity and Claude are listed for
  Enterprise.
- **Profound's prices.** On 2026-11-02 its pricing page showed only a free 7-day Trial and a custom
  Enterprise plan, plus an Agency Growth plan named in its FAQ without a price.
- **Semrush's engines.** Its knowledge base articles disagree: the toolkit article names Google AI Mode and
  ChatGPT for Prompt Tracking, the features article adds AI Overviews, and Perplexity and Gemini appear in
  Brand Performance. The table follows the features article.
- **Ahrefs Brand Radar** is an add-on to a paid Ahrefs plan; the Ahrefs plan itself (from $29 a month,
  Lite $129) is extra.
- **CiteFox's roadmap.** A self-hosted server edition, a managed optimization subscription and an MCP
  server are planned ("coming soon", no dates). The tables show only what ships today.
- **CiteFox's costs.** CiteFox is free; each check uses credits from the services you connect with your
  own API keys, and CiteFox AI runs on an AI assistant you already use or your own API key. See
  [AI-PROVIDERS.md](AI-PROVIDERS.md).
