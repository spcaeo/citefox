## What it shows

A topic is a group of questions about one buyer problem, such as "send texts from Salesforce". The Topics screen lists every topic with its result bar. Open one to see its questions.

## Inside a topic

A topic has five views:

- **List**: every question, one per row.
- **Tree**: questions by buyer stage, then by result.
- **Grid**: stages across, results down. Click any count.
- **Pages**: which of your pages are cited, and which sites win instead.
- **Progress**: the topic over time, once it has more than one check.

The card at the top shows the results and how many page fixes are waiting. In the Grid, **Get fix brief** opens a brief for the questions you are losing.

## How to add a topic

1. Click **New topic**.
2. Describe the buyer problem, or let CiteFox AI suggest one.
3. Review the questions, then pick the target pages, and save.

## Common questions

### How do I set a topic's target page?

Open the topic. Under its name, click **Set target page** (or **Change** next to the one set). You can pick one Primary page and tick **Also OK** on other pages that answer it too.

### Can I put topics in folders?

Yes. Folders only sort your topics. Nothing is checked again.

### Why is the Progress view missing?

It shows once the topic has two or more full checks.
