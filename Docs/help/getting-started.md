## What CiteFox does

CiteFox asks Google and other AI engines the questions your buyers ask. Then it shows whether each AI answer cites your pages, names your brand, or cites someone else.

## How to start

1. **Join CiteFox.** On the first start, type your name and email and say who the reports are for. It is free. Tick the emails you want, or none. Offline? You can still go on.
2. **Make a project.** Open **Projects** and pick **New project**. Type your website.
3. **Let CiteFox read your site.** It finds your pages, your buyers and their questions, and groups the questions into topics.
4. **Run the first check.** CiteFox asks every question and saves the answers. It shows the cost before it starts.
5. **Read the Summary.** See how many questions cite you, and who wins instead.
6. **Fix and repeat.** Open **Fixes** for the pages to improve, log each change, and check again.

## Where things are

- **AI visibility** (left menu): Summary, Questions, Topics, Competitors, Citations, Fixes.
- **Tracking**: Check history, Reports, Alerts & schedules.
- **Pages** and **Settings** sit at the bottom of the menu.
- **Ask** (top bar) answers questions about your results and about CiteFox.
- **Guide** (top bar, for example 6/33) shows short lessons for the screen you are on.
- Press **Ctrl K** (⌘K on a Mac) to find any screen, setting, report or help topic.

## Common questions

### How long does the first check take?

A few minutes for a small project. You can keep working while it runs. The top bar shows its progress.

### Does a check cost money?

Each question uses CiteFox credits. CiteFox shows the cost before a check starts. Set a monthly cap in **Settings › Project settings › Searches & cost**.

### I closed the guide. How do I get it back?

Click **Guide** in the top bar. To start it over, open **Settings › App settings › General**.

### Why does CiteFox ask me to join?

So its makers know who uses it and can tell you about new versions if you ask. Your sites, questions and results never leave this computer.
