## What it shows

One row per check, newest first, grouped by day. Each row shows the page or topic, the market, the results and the searches it used.

Filter by page or topic, market, engine or status. The search box is on the right of the filters row.

## How to compare two checks

1. Tick two checks of the same page or topic.
2. Click **Compare**.
3. The drawer shows the totals side by side, then question by question. **Page content** shows what changed on the page itself.

## Common questions

### Why are there no checks?

Nothing has been checked yet. Add a page or topic, then click **Check now**.

### Can I save the list?

Yes. Tick the checks, then use **Export CSV** (the CSV icon).

### How do I see one page's history?

Open the page, then its **History** tab. It shows events, a timeline and page versions.
