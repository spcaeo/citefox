## What it shows

Head-to-head puts you next to one rival. It reads one check.

- **Head-to-head**: questions you both win, only they win, and only you win.
- **Questions**: the full list.
- **How we measured**.

Pick the rival with **Rival** at the top. It starts with your top competitor.

## Common questions

### Why does it say No other sites cited yet?

The answers cite no other business yet. Run a check, or add competitors on **Competitors**.

### Why can I not pick Reddit as a rival?

Rivals are businesses. Reddit, YouTube and similar sites are not offered.
