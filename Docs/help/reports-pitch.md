## What it shows

The Pitch / audit is short on purpose. It reads one check and shows what a prospect is missing.

Tabs:

- **Summary**: how often they are invisible, cited on the wrong page, or lost to Reddit or YouTube.
- **Competitors**: who wins instead.
- **Plan**: what you would fix first.
- **How we measured**.

On **By goal** this card is called **Pitch a new client** for an agency, **Brief your leadership** in-house, or **One-page audit** with no branding.

## Common questions

### Can I make a pitch for a site that is not my client yet?

Yes. Make a project for their site, run one check, then open the Pitch / audit.

### How do I put my agency name on it?

Set it once in **Settings › App settings › Reports**.
