## What it shows

Pages lists your site's pages with their results. Tabs at the top: **Pages**, **YouTube** and **Communities**.

Saved views:

- **All pages**: every page found on your site.
- **Lost citations**: pages cited before but not now.
- **Tracked pages**: pages CiteFox checks with their own questions.

Switch to the tree to see pages by folder.

## How to add a page

Click **Add a page** and paste its address. CiteFox can write questions for it, or you can type your own.

To add many, use **Import pages** with a CSV file or your sitemap.

## Common questions

### What is the difference between a page and a tracked page?

Every page of your site is listed. A tracked page has its own questions that CiteFox asks on every check.

### How do I save a filtered list?

Set the filters, open **View options** and pick **Save this view…**.

### Can I export the list?

Yes. Open **View options** and pick **Export CSV**.
