## What it shows

Every page the AI answers cite for your questions. Two views:

- **Pages**: each search from the pages side: your pages, the sites cited instead of you, and searches that need no page.
- **List**: every cited page, marked Yours, Competitors or Others.

**Needs review** at the top lists your pages cited for the wrong question, and cited pages of yours CiteFox does not track yet.

## How to pitch a site

When another site is cited next to your competitors, you can ask it to mention you.

1. Find the site's row on a question.
2. Click **✦ Draft a pitch**.
3. CiteFox AI writes a short email. Edit it and send it from your own email.

## Common questions

### What does "Needs review" mean?

CiteFox found something to decide: a page of yours cited where another page should win, or a cited page you do not track. Pick what to do on each row.

### Why are some of my pages missing?

Only pages cited at the latest check show here. Every page you track is on **Pages**.
