## Results

- **Cited**: the AI answer links to one of your pages.
- **Right page**: it links to the page you picked as the target page for that question.
- **Your other page**: it links to one of your pages, but not the target page.
- **Named, not cited**: the answer says your brand name but does not link to you.
- **Not cited**: the answer shows, but you are not in it.
- **No AI Overview**: Google showed no AI answer for that question.
- **Check failed**: CiteFox could not read the answer this time. It tries again on the next check.

## Pages

- **Target page**: the page that should win a question.
- **Primary**: the one main target page for a question.
- **Also OK**: other pages of yours that are fine to cite for that question too. They count as the right page.

## Checks and engines

- **Check**: one round where CiteFox asks every question and saves the answers.
- **AI Overview**: the AI answer at the top of Google results.
- **Engine**: where an answer comes from: Google AI Overview, AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini or Copilot.
- **Market**: a country and language pair, such as US · EN. Answers differ by market.
- **CiteFox credits**: what a check uses. Each question in each market and engine uses some.

## Your project

- **Question**: something a buyer types or asks. A **keyword** is a short search.
- **Topic**: a group of questions about one buyer problem.
- **Competitor**: a business you compete with. Other cited sites (Reddit, YouTube, review sites) show too, but they are not competitors.
- **Fix**: a change you made to a page. CiteFox measures it for 14 days.
- **Score**: the share of checked questions whose answer cites you.

## Common questions

### What is the Right page?

The AI cites the exact page you chose as the target page for that question. If it cites another page of yours, the result is **Your other page**.

### What is the difference between named and cited?

Named means the answer mentions your brand in its text. Cited means it links to your page. A link sends visitors, so cited counts more.

### What are CiteFox credits?

They pay for checks. Each question asked in one market on one engine uses some. The **Status** screen shows what is left.
