## What it shows

Reports has two tabs: **Ready reports** and **Scheduled emails**.

Ready reports can be seen two ways:

- **By goal** (the default): what you need to show. Where do we stand? · Pitch a new client · What should we write next? · Us vs one competitor · What changed? · Did our fixes work?
- **By report**: the six reports by name. Visibility report · What changed · Impact review · Pitch / audit · Content plan · Head-to-head.

Each card says **● Ready**, or what it still needs, such as **○ Needs a 2nd check**.

## How to open, download or send a report

- **Open** shows the report here, with tabs along the top. Some tabs have small sub-tabs under them.
- The download icon saves it as **Offline HTML**, **PDF**, or a table as **CSV**.
- **Print / PDF** prints it.
- **Email on a schedule** sends it to a client after each check.

Inside a report, pick the **Scope** (whole project, one topic or one page) and the check, or the **From** and **To** checks. Search the report with the box on the right.

## Common questions

### Why does a report say Needs a 2nd check?

What changed compares two checks. Run another check, then the report fills in.

### Do the downloaded file and the app show the same numbers?

Yes. Both are built from the same checks.

### Can I put my own name on the reports?

Yes. Open **Settings › App settings › Reports**. See the Reports branding topic.

### Where are the reports I emailed?

Open **Scheduled emails**, then **Delivery history**.
