## What it shows

One row per question or keyword, from every page and topic. Each row shows the latest result (Cited, Named, Not cited, No AI Overview), the page or topic it belongs to, and what changed since the last check.

- Filters sit on the left of the filters row; the search box is on the right.
- Pick an engine logo to see one engine's results.
- Click a question to open its page with the full AI answer.

## How to add questions

1. Click **Add questions**.
2. Type them one per line, or use **✦ Help me write them** to let CiteFox AI suggest questions buyers ask.
3. Pick the topic or page they belong to, and save.

To bring in a list, open ⋯ and pick **Import from Excel or CSV**.

## Common questions

### Why does a question say Not checked?

It was added after the last check. It is asked on the next check, or click **Check now**.

### Why does a question show No AI Overview?

Google showed no AI answer for it in that market. That can change from check to check.

### How do I move questions to another topic?

Tick the rows, then click **Move to topic** in the bar that appears.

### What does Named mean?

The answer says your brand name but does not link to your page. See the glossary for every result word.
