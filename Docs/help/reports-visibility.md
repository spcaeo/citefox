## What it shows

The Visibility report reads one check (the latest, or the one you pick). It answers "Where do we stand?".

Tabs:

- **Overview**: how many questions cite you, how many answer without you, and the biggest chances.
- **Questions**: every question and its result.
- **Competitors**: who is cited instead.
- **Pages**: your cited pages and the questions each one wins.
- **Content**: Topics, Answer shape and Deep dives: what to write.
- **Reputation & sources**: what AI says about you, the sites it trusts, Backlinks, and pages that are Not indexed.
- **How we measured**: the method.

## Common questions

### Which check does it use?

The latest one. Pick another with the **Check** picker at the top.

### Why is a tab greyed?

It has nothing to show yet. Hover it to see what it needs.

### What is the Full report link?

The older full report, for Google AI Overviews only. The Visibility report covers every engine.
