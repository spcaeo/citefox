## What it shows

Open any question to see it in full. Engine tabs at the top switch between Google AI Overview, ChatGPT and the other engines you track.

- **AI Overview**: the answer itself, with your brand and your links marked.
- **Sources**: every site the answer lists.
- **Markets**: the same question in each market.
- **Over time**: the result at each check.
- **Raw data**: the saved search, for experts.

The **Target page** line shows the page that should win, and the page that is cited now.

## How to act on it

- **Copy fix brief** (green button) copies what to change on your page, built from this answer.
- Open ⋯ for **Edit question**, **Check now** and content gaps.
- Use **Change pages** to set the target page.

## Common questions

### Why does Google cite another page of mine?

The AI picked the page that answered best. Make the target page answer the question more directly, or tick the cited page as **Also OK**.

### What does "What the overview says that your page doesn't" mean?

It lists points the AI answer makes that your target page does not cover. Adding them often helps.

### Can I check just this one question?

Yes. Open ⋯ and pick **Check now**.
