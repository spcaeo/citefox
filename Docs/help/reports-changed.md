## What it shows

What changed compares two checks: **From** and **To**. It starts with a verdict, then the details.

Tabs:

- **Verdict**: better, worse or mixed, in one line.
- **What changed**: every question you gained or lost.
- **Questions** and **Competitors**: the full lists for the two checks.
- **Pages**: your cited pages, the ones you published, and any that lost ground.
- **More**: Topics, Deep dives and the Next round.
- **How we measured**.

## Common questions

### Why is What changed empty?

It needs two checks. With one check there is nothing to compare. Run another check and it fills in.

### How do I compare other checks?

Pick them with **From** and **To** at the top.

### What is Lost ground?

Pages that were cited at the From check but are not at the To check.
