## What it shows

The Summary is your project at a glance. Each box is a widget: headline numbers, results at a glance, a trend with markers for your fixes, who gets cited, next fixes and more.

- Pick a **Market** and a **Period** at the top.
- The **Is it working?** line compares now with an earlier check. Use **Compare with** to pick which one.
- Every number opens the list behind it in a drawer.

## How to change the widgets

1. Click **Edit widgets** (pencil icon) at the top right.
2. Move widgets, or remove the ones you do not need.
3. Click **Add widget** to pick more, then **Done**.

Each widget has its own engine filter. Pick an engine logo to see only that engine.

## How to check again

Click **Check now**. CiteFox shows what it will ask and the cost before it starts.

## Common questions

### Why are all the numbers zero?

No check has finished yet. Click **Check now**, or wait for the first check to finish.

### What do the markers on the trend line mean?

A solid marker is a fix you logged. A dashed marker with a question mark is a page change CiteFox saw that nobody logged. Click one to see it.

### How is the score worked out?

Click **How we measure** for the method in plain words. In short: checked questions whose AI answer cites you, out of all checked questions.

### Can I pin a competitor here?

Yes. On Competitors, open a row's ⋯ menu and pick **Pin to Summary**.
