## 0.13.3 — 2026-11-03

### New

- **Typed a name only? CiteFox AI fills in the rest:** when you add a product (in setup or in Settings › General) or a buyer by hand, CiteFox AI writes a short line about it, such as what the product does, and picks its page on your site when it clearly matches one. It shows "✦ Filling in…" while it works, marks its own text with ✦, and keeps "Added by you" with a small "✦ Fill in" link if it is not sure or your AI is not available. It uses only your own AI connection, and anything you type yourself always wins.
- **Join screen:** "Product updates" and "Occasional news and offers" now start ticked. You can untick either one, and change them later in Settings.
- **Report a problem:** if something goes wrong, such as many questions saying "Check failed", open **Help › Report a problem…** (also in Settings › Data & about, and next to a failed check). You see the exact report first: your version, which AI program and keys are set up (yes or no only), the latest failed checks and the end of the log. Send it to the CiteFox team with one click (you get a reference to quote), or copy it, save it as a file, email it to us or open a GitHub issue. CiteFox sends nothing by itself, and keys, emails, IP addresses and your home folder are removed. **Help › Show logs folder** opens the log file.

## 0.13.0 — 2026-11-02

The first public release of CiteFox: a free, local AI visibility tracker. Bring your own API keys; your data stays on your computer.

### New

- **Six client-ready reports:** Visibility, What changed, Impact review, Pitch / audit, Content plan and Head-to-head. Open them by goal or by report; download as offline HTML, PDF or CSV; email them on a schedule. Every number opens the questions behind it.
- **Report branding** for agencies, in-house teams or solo use: your logo and name on every download.
- **Right page cited:** pick a Primary page (plus Also OK pages) for each question and see whether AI answers cite the page you want.
- **Fix tracking:** log a fix and CiteFox shows what it did, with before and after.
- **Reputation and sources:** what AI answers say about your brand, wrong claims to correct, and the Reddit, YouTube and LinkedIn pages they lean on.
- **Help everywhere:** a short guide on every screen, 21 help pages, Ctrl+K to find anything, and Ask CiteFox for how-to questions.
- **Updates:** CiteFox tells you when a new version is out.
- **Downloads for every computer:** one Mac app for every Mac, a Windows installer (no admin needed) and a portable Windows app, and Linux AppImage and deb.

### Improved

- A calmer, cleaner look: one main button per area, tools as icons, search in the same place on every screen.
- Copy and open icons on every address and question.
- Faster checks: several questions run at once.

### Notes

- These builds are not code-signed yet. On a Mac, open CiteFox once, then go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway (or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/CiteFox.app` in Terminal). On Windows, choose More info › Run anyway.

### Changed

- **Topic pages, citation explanations and next fixes (prompt audit P5):** "Why not cited", "Explain this citation" and the Results "Why not cited first" assist now give one diagnosis (new step `topic_citation_diagnosis`, which retires `topic_why_not_cited` and `topic_explain_citation`). "Draft the fix" is its own step (`topic_fix_draft`): steps that name the page's real sections, and an FAQ answer of 40 to 70 words or a section of 60 to 120 words, with no brand in the buyer's question. The topic summary's next three actions are their own step (`topic_next_actions`), run beside the summary, ranked by how close each question is and naming the page path and section. Page match sees each candidate's headings and opening text, with a confidence rubric (below 20 is "no page"), and runs its batches in parallel. The page brief reads the market, the product's competitors, the page's headings and who is cited now. The Progress and Gaps "Draft the fix" and "Suggest the next fix" assists share the same rules and checks. No line may credit a change with a citation.

### Fixed

- **Journey questions:** a stage still short after its retry makes one top-up call for just the missing questions, so compare and decide (which carry the competitor names) no longer end a question short.
- **First-run page counts:** a seed's matching pages ignore filler words (getting, sending, phones, texted…), so one buyer situation no longer "covers" 73 pages through words every page shares.

### Security

- Claude Code runs with tools off (`--tools ""`, `--disallowedTools mcp__*`, plus MCP, safe-mode and session flags where accepted), only from a binary named `claude`, without a shell, in an empty temp folder and its own process group, and never inherits an Anthropic key or OAuth token.
