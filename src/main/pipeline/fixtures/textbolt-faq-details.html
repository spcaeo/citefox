Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between text alerts and SMS marketing campaigns?

Text alerts are SMS messages that notify employees, customers, or vendors about time-sensitive information such as outages, appointment changes, or system alarms. SMS marketing campaigns promote offers, events, and products to subscriber lists, and they need prior express written consent. Alerts usually come from existing systems or an inbox, while campaigns are typically built in a texting platform’s dashboard.

Can a business set up text alerts without writing any code?

Yes, a business can set up text alerts without any code by using an email-to-SMS service. Any system that already sends email, including scheduling software, monitoring tools, and point-of-sale systems, can send an alert by emailing a gateway address. There is no API, SDK, or developer required, and the initial setup takes about 30 minutes.

Where do replies to a text alert go once someone responds?

Replies to a TextBolt alert land in the original sender’s email inbox as a threaded reply. When a customer or staff member texts back, the message is converted to email so the whole conversation stays in Gmail or Outlook. This keeps your inbox as the record of the exchange and means no one has to monitor a separate dashboard to catch responses.
