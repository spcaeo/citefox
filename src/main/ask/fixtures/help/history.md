# Check history

## What it shows

One row per finished check, newest first. Open a row to see its questions.

## Why is What changed empty?

What changed compares two checks. With only one check there is nothing to compare yet, so it stays empty until the next scheduled check finishes. Press **Check now** if you want a second check sooner.
