# Glossary

## Cited

The AI Overview links to your page as one of its sources.

## Right page

A question is on the **right page** when the AI Overview cites the target page you picked for it (Primary or Also OK). If it cites another page of yours, the row says _Other page_.

## Check

One check asks every tracked question in every market once. Each question in each market uses one CiteFox credit.
