Alerts and schedules: when AI citations change

Get told when something important changes after a check.

On this page

  • What it shows
  • How to add an alert
  • Common questions

What it shows

Three tabs:

  • Alert rules: what to watch, where, and who hears.
  • Notifications: where alerts go.
  • Alert log: alerts sent in the last 30 days.

How to add an alert

  1. Click New rule.
  2. Pick what to watch, such as Target page no longer cited, Competitor is cited instead, Score drops by N or more or Check failed.
  3. Pick which pages, and who gets the email. Save.

Common questions

Why did I not get an alert email?

Check that a sending account is set in Settings › App settings › Email, and that the rule is on. The Alert log shows what was sent.

How often are pages checked?

Set it per project in Settings › Project settings › Schedule.

Can I get a report by email instead?

Yes. Use Email on a schedule on any report in Reports.
