Settings: projects, connections and app settings

Project settings, Connections and App settings, and what each tab holds.

On this page

  • Where settings live
  • Project settings
  • App settings
  • Your CiteFox account
  • New versions
  • Common questions

Where settings live

Settings (bottom of the left menu) has three tabs:

  • Project settings: this project only.
  • Connections: your keys for CiteFox credits and CiteFox AI.
  • App settings: every project on this computer.

Project settings

  • General: name, domain and brand.
  • What to track: which engines to check.
  • Markets: countries and languages.
  • Schedule: how often pages are checked again.
  • Searches & cost: the monthly cap on credits.
  • CiteFox AI: the AI that writes questions and briefs.
  • Project rules: words to always or never use.
  • Templates: question templates and buyer profile.
  • Reports: your client's name and logo on this project's reports.
  • Data: export, duplicate or delete the project.

App settings

  • AI & searches: defaults for every project.
  • General: celebrations, the guide, tips and sounds.
  • Email: the sending account for reports and alerts.
  • Reports: who reports are from, and your logo.
  • Data & about: your CiteFox account, version, backup and import.

Your CiteFox account

Settings › App settings › Data & about › Your CiteFox account holds the details you gave at Join CiteFox: name, email, company and who the reports are for. Tick or untick Product updates and Occasional news and offers here, then Save. "Not sent yet" means you were offline; CiteFox sends it later.

Your SEO data stays on this computer. Only these details and an anonymous daily version check are sent.

New versions

When a new CiteFox is out, a strip says so at the top: What's new opens the notes, Download gets the installer for your computer. Install it over the old one; your data is kept. The footer shows a dot until you update. To look now, open the ⚙ menu and pick Check for updates.

Common questions

Where do I add an API key?

Open Settings › Connections.

How do I check less often to save credits?

Open Project settings › Schedule and pick a longer interval, or set a cap in Searches & cost.

How do I stop CiteFox emails?

Untick both boxes in Your CiteFox account, or use the unsubscribe link in any email.

Where do I change the theme?

Use the theme menu in the top bar: Light, Dark, Sepia or System.
