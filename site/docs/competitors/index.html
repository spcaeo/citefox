Competitors: who AI answers cite instead of you

Who AI answers cite for your questions, and where they beat you.

On this page

  • What it shows
  • How to add a competitor
  • Common questions

What it shows

Every site the AI answers cite or name for your questions, with how often and on which questions. Saved competitors come first. Other sites (Reddit, YouTube, review sites, media) are grouped by kind of site.

Click a competitor to see the questions where it is cited and you are not.

How to add a competitor

  • Click Add competitor and type its name and domain.
  • Or open ⋯ and pick Help me find competitors, or Import competitors from a file.

Common questions

The same company shows twice. Can I join them?

Yes. Open the row's ⋯ menu and pick Merge with…. One name stays, with every domain.

Why is Reddit not a competitor?

Reddit, YouTube and review sites are places buyers read, not businesses you compete with. They show under their kind of site, so you can still see them.

How do I beat a competitor on a question?

Open the competitor, then a question it wins. Log a fix after you change your page, and CiteFox measures it.
