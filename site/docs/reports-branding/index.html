Report branding: your agency name and logo

Put your agency or company name and logo on every report.

On this page

  • Who reports are from
  • How to add your logo
  • The client's name and logo
  • Common questions

Who reports are from

Open Settings › App settings › Reports and answer Who are these reports from?:

  • An agency, for clients: your agency's name, logo, website and contact email, with "Prepared for" each client.
  • My own company (in-house): your company's name and logo, for your team or leadership.
  • Just me, no branding: plain reports, no logo.

You set this once and it is used for every project. You can also click Edit branding on the Reports home.

How to add your logo

  1. Open Settings › App settings › Reports.
  2. Click Add logo and pick a PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG file, up to 150 KB.
  3. Click Save.

The logo stays on this computer.

The client's name and logo

Each project can show its own client. Open Settings › Project settings › Reports and fill in Client name and the client's logo.

Common questions

How do I add my agency logo?

Open Settings › App settings › Reports, pick An agency, for clients, then Add logo and Save.

Why does my report show the site's icon?

In-house reports with no logo use your site's icon. Add a logo to replace it.

Does branding change the numbers?

No. It only changes the name, logo and "Prepared for" line.
