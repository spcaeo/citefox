Fixes: fix briefs, changes and 14-day verdicts

What to change on your pages, the changes you made, and whether they worked.

On this page

  • What it shows
  • How to log a fix
  • Common questions

What it shows

Fixes is the loop: fix a page, log it, see what it did, repeat. Three tabs:

  • What to fix: page fixes, biggest first. Each says which page, how many searches it can win and its win odds. Kinds: Improve a page, Pick which page owns it, Write a new page.
  • Changes made: every fix you logged.
  • Did it work?: each fix's result after 14 days live.

How to log a fix

  1. Change the page on your site.
  2. Click Log a fix, pick the page, say what you changed and the day it went live.
  3. CiteFox measures the questions it should help for 14 days.

From a page fix, I published this logs it for you.

Common questions

Why does a fix say Too early?

A fix needs 14 days live before CiteFox gives a verdict. It shows how many days are left.

What do Worked, No change and Got worse mean?

They compare the fix's questions before and after it went live. "Less certain" means a Google update happened in the same window.

Why is What to fix empty?

Either every question already cites the right page, or no check has finished yet.

What is a fix brief?

A short note of what to change on a page so it answers the questions better. Copy it for your writer.
