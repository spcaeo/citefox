Impact review: before and after your fixes

What your published pages and fixes did, as a before and after.

What it shows

The Impact review answers "Did our fixes work?". It follows the pages you published or changed, and compares their questions before and after.

Tabs:

  • Verdict: what your work did, in one line.
  • Published pages: each page you logged and its result.
  • Questions and Competitors.
  • Pages: your cited pages and any that lost ground.
  • Client asks: what to ask the client for: indexing, links and the next round.
  • More: every move, Topics and Deep dives.
  • How we measured.

Common questions

Why does it say Needs a logged fix?

The Impact review follows logged fixes. Go to Fixes and click Log a fix for each page you changed.

Can I send it to a client every two weeks?

Yes. In Scheduled emails, pick the ready-made Impact review every 14 days.

What are Client asks?

Things only the client can do, such as getting a page indexed or adding a link to it.
