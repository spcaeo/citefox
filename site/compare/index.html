CiteFox vs Profound, Peec AI, Otterly.AI and other AI visibility tools

Looking for a free, local alternative to Profound, Peec AI, Otterly.AI or Semrush’s AI toolkit? Here is an honest, sourced comparison: what each tool tracks, what it costs to start, and where each one leads. CiteFox is not affiliated with any of these companies.

Why teams pick CiteFox

  • Free and local

    A free desktop app. Your data on your computer, your own keys, the cost shown before every check.

  • The right page, not just the domain

    Set the page that should be cited for each question; CiteFox tells you if that page, another of yours, your brand without a link, or someone else was cited.

  • Fixes with a verdict

    Log a change and CiteFox re-checks for 14 days, then says whether it worked, made no change or got worse.

  • Six client-ready reports

    Visibility, What changed, Impact review, Pitch / audit, Content plan and Head-to-head, rebuilt after every check.

  • Branded for agencies

    Your agency’s name and logo with “Prepared for” each client, or your company’s, or none.

  • Every number drills down

    Click any number, in the app or a report, to see the exact questions and answers behind it.

Where others go further, and why CiteFox is built differently

  • Hosted dashboards. The others run in a browser with shared logins. CiteFox keeps everything on your computer instead: no seat fees, no client data in someone else's cloud, and branded reports to share. A self-hosted server edition is coming soon for teams that want one shared place.
  • More engines. Some enterprise plans add Grok, DeepSeek, Meta AI or Mistral. CiteFox focuses on the six engines where buyers ask today, and checks each one in depth: the answer, every source, your position and the right page.
  • Prompt volumes. Ahrefs, Profound, Peec AI, Promptwatch and Semrush estimate how often people ask a prompt. Those are models: no AI platform except Copilot shares real query data, and chat assistants answer each person differently because of memory. CiteFox measures real answers to the buyer questions you choose, anchored on Google AI Overviews (why).
  • AI crawler and traffic analytics. Several tools read server or CDN logs inside their cloud. CiteFox stays focused on AI answers, and pairs with Skald, our free analytics that runs on your own server: every AI crawler and AI assistant visit by name, verified, split by why it came, plus the people and leads AI answers sent you. Both keep your data with you.
  • APIs and enterprise extras. Others offer APIs, SSO and dedicated support. With CiteFox your data is already yours, in a local database you can query, export or back up; an MCP server for AI agents is coming soon.
  • Content writing agents. Some tools write pages for you. CiteFox gives you a precise brief for the right page, so your writers stay in control of your voice and facts.

Who should use what

  • Page-level answers, fix verdicts and branded client reports, with no subscription and the data on your own computer: CiteFox.
  • Many engines, prompt volumes, crawler logs, SSO and a dedicated team, for a large brand: Profound, or an Enterprise plan of Peec AI or Scrunch.
  • A hosted, self-serve tracker with daily runs and a low entry price: Otterly.AI or Peec AI.
  • An agent that also writes and publishes content: Promptwatch or Searchable.
  • AI visibility beside your keyword and backlink data: the Semrush or Ahrefs add-on you already pay for.
  • Both: CiteFox sits beside any of these. A hosted tool can watch the whole category daily while CiteFox checks which of your pages is cited and whether each fix worked.

Common questions

What is the difference between CiteFox and Profound?

Profound is a hosted platform for large brands, with up to 9 engines on its Enterprise plan, prompt volumes, AI crawler analytics, an API and agents. CiteFox is a free desktop app: your data stays on your computer, it checks whether the page you want cited is the one cited, and it gives each logged fix a 14-day verdict.

Is CiteFox a free alternative to Peec AI or Otterly.AI?

CiteFox is free to download and use; checks run on your own API keys or credits, with the cost shown before each check, and your data stays on your computer. Peec AI starts at $95 a month and Otterly.AI at $29 a month (list prices on 2026-11-02), both as hosted dashboards with daily runs, which CiteFox does not have today.

Which AI visibility tool tracks the most engines?

Promptwatch, Scrunch, AthenaHQ and the Enterprise plans of Profound and Peec AI track more engines than CiteFox, which tracks six: Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Copilot.
